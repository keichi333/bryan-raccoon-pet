# Clean anime gaze mechanics

Keep paws, feet, front jacket hem, green satchel on screen-left and tail on screen-right planted. The neck is visibly embedded in the scarf; the head turns with a tiny coherent shoulder follow-through, never as a floating detached object. Pompom, charcoal beanie, yellow cuff and ears move with the head. Do not roll/tilt the head sideways.

Keep the canonical dark rounded eyes inside black eye masks, with the same tiny clean white highlights. No new white googly eyeballs, floating pupils or changed eye design. Pitch/yaw the entire face, eyes and muzzle naturally.

UP: lift chin and nose, reveal pale underside of muzzle, eyes aim toward upper image edge, still broadly frontal. RIGHT: head turns only toward screen-right, nose clearly right of head center, far cheek compresses; front torso remains fixed. DOWN: chin tucks toward scarf, nose below eye line, upper beanie/forehead more visible, dark eyes remain visible looking downward. LEFT: inverse of right, nose projects toward screen-left and green bag, never the character's own left.

The sixteen poses form one continuous clockwise sweep up → upper-right → right → lower-right → down → lower-left → left → upper-left → up, with evenly changing yaw and pitch. Cardinal anchors must be unmistakable. No entire-body turntable, no back view, no scaling, torso rotation, bag/tail side changes, or neck snapping. Crisp anime ink and cel color blocks remain identical in all directions.
