# Natural gaze mechanics

The paws, feet, jacket hem, attached satchel and tail stay planted in the same front-facing neutral position. The neck turns the head with slight upper-shoulder follow-through; the beanie, ears and pompom follow the head. The eyes retain the original dark, wet, rounded eye surfaces and highlights inside their raccoon masks, with eyelids following physical head pitch. Never add white googly eyes or slide isolated replacement pupils.

UP: raise chin and nose visibly, show underside of muzzle, eyes and brow oriented toward top edge; face still visible. DOWN: tuck chin, nose below face center, brow/beanie overlap upper face slightly; eyes visibly lowered, not disappeared. RIGHT/LEFT: turn only head moderately toward that screen edge, nose extends clearly into that half, near cheek broadens and far cheek compresses; retain both eyes as appropriate and front torso.

Diagonal poses combine head yaw and pitch in even clockwise steps. The continuous loop follows up → up-right → right → down-right → down → down-left → left → up-left → up. This is looking around nervously, never rotating the whole raccoon or showing its back. Tail and satchel must not switch sides.
