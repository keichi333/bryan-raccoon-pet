# Built-in imagegen prompt set

All artwork used the built-in image_gen tool. The generated job prompts are saved under prompts/. Every later visual job included the original reference, canonical base, layout guide and required approved references. No pet pixels were drawn procedurally.

The base prompt retained: small skinny gray-brown raccoon, round wet eyes, dark eye masks, cream muzzle, charcoal pompom beanie with mustard cuff, oversized worn dark teal jacket, mustard scarf, attached green first-aid satchel with pale cross on screen-left, ringed tail on screen-right; fine inked storybook style, frightened innocent expression and hunched shoulders; flat #FF00FF backdrop; no scenery, UI, text, floating effects or shadows.

For look-row-9, the generated prompt was supplemented with: fixed feet, front torso, paws gripping scarf, green satchel on screen-left and tail on screen-right; physical head/beanie/ears turn and pitch; preserve dark wet eyes and masks; up exposes underside of chin, right moves nose screen-right, down tucks chin to scarf.

The first look-row-10 was rejected for facing screen-right. The accepted regeneration added the following emphasis before the generated prompt:

CRITICAL DIRECTION CORRECTION: Draw the LEFT HALF of the gaze loop. In frames 2 through 8, every nose/muzzle and gaze must point toward the LEFT EDGE OF THE IMAGE, toward the green satchel, as in the LARGE LEFT-FACING ANCHOR at upper-left of reference3 and the FOURTH pose of reference4. The attached completed row9 looks RIGHT; use row9 ONLY for body size and style, DO NOT COPY its head orientation. Frame5 is an unmistakable LEFT-FACING HEAD, nose on LEFT side of face. Torso, satchel and tail remain fixed, not mirrored.

It also specified: Reference3 combines enlarged approved LEFT anchor (upper-left), standard contact sheet (center) and invisible eight-slot layout guide (bottom); copy no labels or guide marks. Draw EXACTLY 8 coherent full-body separated frames: DOWN; DOWN-LEFT slight; DOWN-LEFT; LEFT slightly DOWN; LEFT; LEFT slightly UP; UP-LEFT; UP slightly LEFT. Face turns toward LEFT IMAGE BORDER in frames2-8. Keep original large dark wet eyes and markings, nose naturally yaw/pitch. Bag stays LEFT, tail RIGHT. Flat magenta #FF00FF, no effects.

Reference collage was required to fit the image generator's five-reference limit; it contains all manifest references without redrawing the character.
